Dialogue Classifier 2.0 - System Overview

Dialogue Classifier 2.0 is an enterprise-grade NLP text classification platform. Upgraded from an initial 6-class Tkinter script, v2.0 introduces multi-sentence segmentation, regex word-boundary pattern matching, a trained TF-IDF Scikit-Learn machine learning pipeline (comparing Logistic Regression and Multinomial Naive Bayes), a hybrid consensus resolver, lexicon-contextual sentiment scoring, 6-class emotion detection, SQLite audit telemetry, batch CSV processing, and an interactive SaaS dashboard.

2. Supported Dialogue Categories

GREETING

Social opening salutations & inquiries (e.g. "Hello", "Good morning", "Hey").

FAREWELL

Departure rituals & well-wishes (e.g. "Goodbye", "Take care", "See you later").

QUESTION

Inquiries seeking information (e.g. "What time?", "Where is it?").

REQUEST

Polite directives asking for action (e.g. "Could you please review?", "Kindly send").

EXCLAMATION

High-intensity emotional interjections (e.g. "Wow!", "Awesome job!").

STATEMENT

Informative assertions and declarations (e.g. "The report is published.").

COMPLAINT

Expressions of customer dissatisfaction or defects (e.g. "This keeps crashing").

SUGGESTION

Proposals and advice (e.g. "You should clear cookies", "Why don't we try?").

APOLOGY

Expressions of regret (e.g. "I am sorry for the delay", "Pardon me").

THANKS

Expressions of gratitude (e.g. "Thank you very much", "Much appreciated").

3. Classification Modes

  • Hybrid Classification (Recommended): Dynamically synthesizes rule-based patterns and machine learning probability confidence with transparent conflict resolution.
  • Enhanced Rule-Based Engine: 10-class regex word-boundary matcher with multi-sentence intent ranking and pattern extraction.
  • Original Rule-Based Engine: 100% faithful backward-compatible preservation of the original v1.0 logic from dialogue_classifier.py.
  • Machine Learning: Pure Scikit-Learn TF-IDF vectorization with true probability distributions (never synthetic numbers).
